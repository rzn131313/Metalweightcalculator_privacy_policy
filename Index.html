Summary: Metal Weight Calculator is built with privacy in mind. Your metal calculations, dimensions, custom prices, and calculation history are processed and saved entirely on your local device. We do not operate any external database to store or track your personal information.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Metal Weight Calculator ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent data practices in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Information Collection and Storage

A. Local Calculation Data (Stored On-Device Only):

  • Metal Dimensions & Calculations: All lengths, widths, diameters, densities, weights, and price calculations you input remain strictly on your device using a local SQLite/Room database.
  • User Preferences: Selected currency symbol, theme mode, and default material selections are stored locally in your device's private App Preferences.
  • No Cloud Sync / No Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide your name, phone number, or email address to use the core calculator functions.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Service Providers

To support the development and maintenance of this free application, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob (a service of Google LLC).

A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & Google Play Services:

AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous data for ad serving, analytics, and fraud prevention as governed by Google's privacy policies. This may include:

  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used by Google Play Services to serve relevant ads and frequency capping.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, carrier, network connection type (Wi-Fi / Mobile), and language settings.
  • Approximate Location: Derived from general network/IP location (not precise GPS) to display localized ads.
  • Ad Interaction Telemetry: Clicks, impressions, and performance diagnostics to detect invalid traffic and maintain platform integrity.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review Google's official privacy documentation:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services

4. Android Device Permissions

The app requests only minimal, standard permissions strictly required for functionality and advertising:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Allows the app to connect to Google servers to load advertising creatives and updates.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Enables checking whether an active network connection exists before requesting ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Standard Google Play Services permission allowing Google AdMob to access the Advertising ID in compliance with Android 12+ policies.

5. Data Retention & User Rights (Data Deletion)

Because all calculation history and user preferences are stored exclusively on your device:

  • You can delete your entire calculation history at any time directly in the app by tapping the "Clear History" button in the History tab.
  • You can permanently remove all stored application data by clearing app storage in your Android system settings (Settings > Apps > Metal Weight Calculator > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the app.
  • You can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your phone settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is an engineering tool intended for students, engineers, fabricators, and general audiences. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take appropriate steps to address it.

7. Security

We value your trust. The app does not transmit your engineering figures or commercial estimates to any remote server. Ad communications are transmitted securely using standard industry protocols (HTTPS/TLS) managed by the official Google Mobile Ads SDK.
